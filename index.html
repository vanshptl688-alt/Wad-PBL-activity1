Essential Safety Tips

Suspicious Activity Checklist

If you notice suspicious activity:

  1. Stop interacting with the suspicious message or website.
  2. Do not share passwords or OTPs.
  3. Change compromised passwords.
  4. Enable two-factor authentication.
  5. Report the incident to the relevant platform or cybercrime authority.
